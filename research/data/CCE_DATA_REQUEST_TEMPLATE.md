# Request for rice crop-cutting yield data

**Subject:** Request for anonymized rice crop-cutting experiment data and documentation for academic research

Dear Director / Public Information Officer,

I am preparing an academic study of rice-yield estimation and model evaluation. I would like to ask whether your Directorate can provide anonymized data or published summaries from the General Crop Estimation Survey (GCES), Crop Estimation Survey (CES), or crop-cutting experiments (CCE) for rice/paddy in [Andhra Pradesh / Telangana] for the available years and seasons.

I do not need names, phone numbers, addresses, or other personal identifiers. If plot-level records cannot be shared, district- or mandal-season estimates with sample counts and uncertainty metadata would also be useful.

If available, could you please provide:

1. Records or estimates with district/mandal, crop, season, agricultural year, and the yield outcome with its exact unit and definition.
2. For plot-level CCE data, plot area, harvested weight, moisture or drying adjustment, experiment date, and any irrigation/input fields that are approved for release.
3. Sample sizes, experiment counts, sampling weights or selection probabilities, the sampling design, and standard errors or other precision measures.
4. Data dictionaries, code lists, missing-value conventions, quality-control procedures, and the boundary/geography vintage used for each year.
5. The source lineage of these records relative to the Directorate's official Area, Production and Yield (APY) statistics: specifically, whether the APY yield series is calculated from these same CCE records or from overlapping samples.
6. The available years, any changes in collection or processing, and the terms or license for research use, publication, and redistribution.

CSV or another machine-readable format is preferred. If the data are not publicly downloadable, please let me know the applicable access procedure and whether a formal application is required. I will acknowledge the source and follow the conditions you specify.

Thank you for your time.
